Can You Recognize the Scam?

Realistic situations can help you identify warning signs before you lose money.

📱

Scenario 1: UPI Request

A stranger says they accidentally sent you money and asks you to scan a QR code to receive a refund.


Warning:

Scanning a QR code for receiving money is unnecessary. A payment QR code can be used to make a payment.

🏦

Scenario 2: Bank Call

Someone calls claiming to be from your bank and says your account will be blocked unless you provide an OTP.


Warning:

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with callers.

💼

Scenario 3: Fake Job

You receive a message offering a work-from-home job with a high salary. They ask for a registration fee.


Warning:

Verify the company and job independently before paying any money.

🎁

Scenario 4: Prize Message

A message says you won a large prize but must pay a processing fee before receiving it.


Warning:

Unexpected prizes that require upfront payments are a common warning sign.

🔍

Stop. Verify. Then Act.

Scammers often create urgency so victims act without thinking.
