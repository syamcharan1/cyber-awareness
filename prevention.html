How to Stay Safe Online

Small safety habits can protect your money, accounts and personal information.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and avoid using the same password everywhere.

🔢

Never Share OTP

Banks and legitimate services should not require you to reveal your OTP to another person.

🔗

Check Links

Don't open suspicious links received through unknown emails, SMS or social media.

💳

Verify Payments

Check the receiver's name and payment details before approving a transaction.

🔒

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever it is available for your accounts.

📞

Verify Callers

Do not trust a caller just because they know your name or some personal information.

📲

Install Carefully

Download apps from trusted sources and review permissions before installing.

🧠

Don't Act in a Hurry

Scammers create fear and urgency. Take time to verify information.

💡

Remember the Golden Rule

Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV or password with anyone.
