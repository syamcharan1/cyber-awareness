Common Cyber Crimes

Learn about common online scams and how criminals try to steal money, passwords and personal information.

📱

UPI Fraud

Fraudsters may send fake payment requests or convince victims to scan a QR code.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, websites and messages are designed to steal login details and financial information.

🔢

OTP Fraud

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees and ask for an OTP or verification code.

💼

Job Scams

Fake job offers may ask applicants to pay registration, training or security fees.

📈

Investment Scams

Fraudsters may promise extremely high returns and pressure people to invest quickly.

🛒

Online Shopping Fraud

Fake shopping websites or social media sellers may take payment without delivering products.

📞

Fake Customer Care

Criminals may pretend to be customer support agents and ask for sensitive information.

📲

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, giveaways and messages may be used to collect money or personal details.

🏦

Banking Fraud

Attackers may use fake calls or messages to obtain banking credentials.

🪪

Identity Theft

Personal information may be misused to impersonate another person.

📩

Lottery Scams

Victims may receive fake messages claiming they won a prize and must pay a fee.

💰

Loan App Scams

Fraudulent loan applications may collect personal information or demand unreasonable payments.

⚠️

When in doubt, don't pay.

Verify the person, website or payment request before taking action.
