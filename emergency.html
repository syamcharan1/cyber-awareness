What To Do If You Lose Money?

Act quickly. The sooner you report a financial cyber fraud, the better.

🛑

Step 1: Stop

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Do not send additional money.

🏦

Step 2: Contact Your Bank

Immediately contact your bank or payment service through its official customer support.

📸

Step 3: Save Evidence

Keep screenshots, transaction details, phone numbers, emails and messages.

📢

Step 4: Report

Report the incident through the appropriate official cybercrime reporting channels in your country.

🔐

Step 5: Secure Accounts

Change compromised passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

👨‍👩‍👧

Step 6: Inform Others

Tell family or friends if the scam could affect them too. Sharing awareness can prevent further victims.

⚠️

Do Not Blame Yourself

Cyber scams are designed to manipulate people. Report the incident and take steps to secure your accounts.
