Common Cyber Crimes

Cyber criminals use different methods to steal money and personal information.

🎣

Phishing

Fake websites, emails and messages are used to steal information.

🔢

OTP Fraud

Criminals pretend to be bank employees and ask for OTPs.

💼

Job Scams

Fake jobs are used to collect money or personal information.
